THE CHECKS BEHIND EACH REVIEW

How it works

A rules-based first pass. No payment is made, and fuzzy matches always wait for a person.

01
Read the document

Extract the invoice ID, date, supplier, PO reference, currency, and amount from selectable PDF text.

INPUT
02
Check identity & history

Fingerprint duplicate submissions. Normalize supplier names. Similar names suggest a human check; they do not silently pass.

IDENTITY
03
Reconcile the commitment

Match the PO, approved status, currency, amount tolerance, subtotal/tax arithmetic, and previously approved billing.

CONTROL
✓Ready for hand-offClean match; a person still approves.
◷Needs a second lookIncomplete, unclear, or outside the agreed tolerance.
↗Stopped for a reasonDuplicate submission or supplier mismatch.
✳
A FEW CLEAR LIMITS

This first pass will not

  • Send or initiate a payment
  • Auto-match a fuzzy supplier name
  • Read text from a scanned image
  • Approve spending above the PO
  • Hide the reason for a decision
Field coverage counts what was extracted. It does not override a failed check.
