INTERNAL CONTROL ARCHITECTURE

How Mosaic AP Works

A deterministic, evidence-backed accounts payable pipeline designed for auditability and fraud prevention.

01 Intake & OCR

Extracts fields, line items, and detects digital text vs scanned images.

→
02 Identity & Duplicates

Cryptographic SHA-256 fingerprinting and historical collision detection.

→
03 PO Commitment

Reconciles PO authorization, approved status, and cumulative spending cap.

→
04 3-Way Matching

Compares PO lines vs Goods Receipt Notes (GRNs) vs Invoice quantities & unit prices.

→
05 Financial Math

Validates subtotal + tax = total, currency consistency, and payment terms.

→
06 Risk Scoring Engine

Calculates 0-100 explainable risk score with transparent point contributions.

→
07 Human Governance

Reviewer disposition with required rationale, feedback logging, and immutable audit trail.

✓ What Mosaic AP Does Automatically
  • Extracts invoice line items, SKUs, quantities, and rates from text PDFs.
  • Reconciles line items against PO lines and Goods Receipt Notes (GRNs).
  • Flags quantity discrepancies where invoiced units exceed goods received.
  • Calculates an explainable 0-100 risk score with explicit contributing factors.
  • Detects duplicate submissions and reference collisions across vendors.
  • Maintains an immutable audit trail of reviewer notes, overrides, and corrections.
✕ Strict Governance Boundaries (What Mosaic Never Does)
  • Never initiates or executes a bank disbursement or wire transfer.
  • Never auto-approves invoices with fuzzy supplier matches.
  • Never guesses numbers or text from image-only scans without OCR.
  • Never silently overrides purchase order budgets or approved tolerances.
  • Never allows a model recommendation override without a recorded rationale.
  • Never hallucinates AI facts; all assistant answers cite structured evidence.
